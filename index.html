About Material

鎂鋁鋅：會自我修復的防鏽鋼材Zn-Al-Mg: Self-Healing Corrosion Protection

支架切割後，切割面起初會出現紅鏽。隨著鍍層中的鎂（Mg）與鋅（Zn）逐漸形成一層穩定的保護膜 Simonkolleite（Zn₅(OH)₈Cl₂·H₂O），將切割面包覆住，達到防鏽效果。 Cut edges may show red rust at first. Over time, the magnesium (Mg) and zinc (Zn) in the coating form a stable protective layer called Simonkolleite (Zn₅(OH)₈Cl₂·H₂O), which seals the cut edge and stops corrosion.
